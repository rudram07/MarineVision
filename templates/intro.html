The Marine Debris Problem

Underwater environments suffer from severely limited optical visibility. Consequently, marine scientists rely on Side-Scan Sonar (SSS) to map the seabed. However, manual inspection of gigabytes of acoustic imagery is:

  • Acoustically Complex: High speckle noise, acoustic shadows, beam attenuation, and seabed ripple clutter make subtle debris targets hard to distinguish.
  • Labor-Intensive: Manual review of thousands of waterfall scans requires specialized hydrographic expertise and creates human fatigue.
  • Inconsistent: Visual interpretation of irregular debris objects varies between observers and lacks standardized analytical records.

Proposed Automated Solution

MarineVision delivers an automated software pipeline engineered to process raw SSS imagery, standardize backscatter intensities, and prepare the foundation for neural detection:

  • Bilateral & CLAHE Preprocessing: Eliminates speckle noise while preserving object-shadow contrast boundaries.
  • Modular AI Detectors: Built-in extensible architecture designed for YOLO debris localization and Autoencoder anomaly scoring.
  • Relational Audit Trail: Stores all image metadata, operational runs, and parameters in an ACID-compliant SQLite database.
  • Automated PDF Reports: Generates publication-ready scientific reports on-demand using ReportLab.
